## Notes

- **pdf-lib** — the minified bundle `pdf-lib.min.js` also contains code from its declared dependencies
  `@pdf-lib/standard-fonts`, `@pdf-lib/upng`, `pako` and `tslib`. Of these, only the `tslib` notice survives inside
  the bundle; the other three have no notice in the minified file, so their copyright lines and license texts are
  reproduced in the "Code embedded in the bundles" section below.
- **fontkit** — the `@pdf-lib/fontkit` 1.1.1 package does not include a LICENSE file. Its `package.json` declares
  MIT; `vendor/fontkit/LICENSE` is the standard MIT text with the authors named in that `package.json`. The minified
  bundle `fontkit.umd.min.js` is a Rollup build that inlines many packages, and none of their notices survive in it;
  they are listed and reproduced in the next section.
- **PDF.js** — `pdf.min.js` and `pdf.worker.min.js` are the prebuilt files from `pdfjs-dist`, unchanged. The
  document is always opened with `isEvalSupported: false` (CVE-2024-4367).
- **Literata** — the four TTF files are unchanged. `vendor/literata/literata-*.js` are base64 encodings of the same
  TTF files (generated by `araclar/yazitipi-js-uret.mjs`), so that the fonts load from `file://` as well. The fonts
  are embedded into the PDFs the app generates from EPUB files, which the OFL permits.
- **Colorfigures** — the Learn tab's drawings use a modified copy of the Colorfigures engine
  (`js/ogren/cf-motor.js`, `js/ogren/cf-sayfa.js`), figures made with its method (`js/ogren/sekil/`) and styles
  (`css/ogren.css`); every one of these files starts with the MIT notice. Upstream credits: Colorfigures is built on
  ISO-GLOW by April Zhu (https://isoglow.dev, MIT), which is built on iso-figure by Tolga Cohce
  (https://github.com/MrBongoC/ai-iso-skill, MIT). The engine's "Made with Colorfigures" link is turned off
  (`credit: false`) because the app makes no external requests; the attribution is shown in the Learn tab (with
  the full MIT text), in this file and in the README.
